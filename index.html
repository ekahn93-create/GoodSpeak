Punctuation Rules

Review and practice punctuation marks

;
Semicolon
Links two independent clauses that are closely related
"I love reading; it expands my mind."
:
Colon
Introduces a list, explanation, or quotation
"She has three hobbies: reading, writing, and painting."
—
Em Dash
Emphasizes or sets apart information
"The solution—simple yet elegant—solved the problem."
,
Oxford Comma
Final comma in a list before "and" or "or"
"I bought apples, oranges, and bananas."

Elevator Pitch Builder

Craft concise, compelling introductions

30-Second Pitch Structure:

  1. Who you are (name, role)
  2. What you do (value proposition)
  3. Why it matters (benefit/impact)
  4. Call to action (next step)

Example:

"Hi, I'm [Name], a [Role] at [Company/Industry]. I help [Target Audience] [Solve Problem/Achieve Goal] by [Your Solution/Approach]. This matters because [Impact/Benefit]. I'd love to [Next Step - connect, discuss, share more]."
